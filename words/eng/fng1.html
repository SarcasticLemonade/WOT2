🛖 Chapter 1 — The Lion

1 The sage said at a village congregation,
2 “Evil are the Eristonneans who come into our homes,
3 ask not any permission,
4 and take us for concubines and slaves as if we are merely things and not people!”
5 And the village congregation exclaimed,
6 “She said that evil are the Eristonneans!”
7 And a baker—an Eristonnean, cried out,
8 “So do you think that I too am evil?”
9 and he threw a fresh loaf at her.
10 All this was seen by a bunch Eristonnean policemen,
11 who quickly took the sage for imprisonment,
12 but on the way there was a lion who fell enraged and killed all the policemen,
13 and the sage could run towards safety and survive.
14 Indeed is earth sublime.
