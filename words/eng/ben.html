🧬 Li 1

1 Life is the time during which a person exists upon
2 the face of earth. Life is all earth, all good and evil,
3 and all time. Life is all that one is
4 to know, do and know. There is no
5 time outside your life, but to earth and other persons
6 there exists time outside your life. Life is a gift
7 greater than all gifts, for life gives
8 the might to come upon goodness, and it gives unto
9 you the might to come upon evils so that you
10 might rejoice.
