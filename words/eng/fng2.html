🕯 Second Word of the Sage 1

1 The sage said, “Come not
2 upon the day when wars begin over that which
3 is not; come not upon the day when
4 children sell their minds away for flesh before
5 they know how to speak; come not
6 upon the day when it is called a sin to love;
7 come not upon the day when the brightest
8 of the brightest know not what a tree is,”
9 said the sage.

🕯 Second Word of the Sage 2

1 The sage said, “As earth does
2 to woman, thus woman will do to earth;
3 as earth does to woman, thus woman will do
4 to deer; for through loving one thanks
5 those who love her; through hating one shows
6 ingratitude to those who love her,” said
7 the sage.

🕯 Second Word of the Sage 4

1 The sage said, “In our
2 struggle towards goodness, let us strive wisely and
3 think about the future, about our
4 children and the children of theirs,
5 for we shan't be like lions of
6 the land, who only aspire towards one point and
7 never see beyond it; instead let us be like
8 birds of the sky, who set out towards
9 one direction and always go after it; who go
10 further and further towards lands fuller and fuller
11 of goodness,” said the sage.
