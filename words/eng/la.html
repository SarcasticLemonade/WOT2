❤️ Lv 1

1 There are three kinds of love: there is familial love,
2 there is neighbourly love and there is romantic love.
3 Familial love is the desire of all members
4 of a family to help one another and to do
5 good. Familial love must at all times exist unless
6 one member of the family has committed a grave evil against them.
7 One must have and give neighbourly love unto every
8 person upon the face of earth, benefactor and malefactor,
9 layman and weirdo. Romantic love one
10 must give unto one person throughout all of their life, for
11 this bond is such that one can
12 never recreate it with someone else after one has
13 tied it with someone.

❤️ Lv 2

1 A lover is a person who loves and is loved.
2 One's lover is whosoever loves one and by
3 one is loved.

❤️ Lv 3

1 When you wish to do evils unto someone,
2 do good instead; when a child has broken
3 something by accident, punish them not, but tell
4 them how to prevent it in the future;
5 when someone has what you envy, steal
6 it not from them, but stop and
7 think on how to resolve the envy and forgive
8 them.

❤️ Lv 4

1 If one partner loves not the soul of the other partner
2 but their body, this is not love,
3 for romantic love adores the whole person and a whole
4 person is a soul inside a body.

❤️ Lv 5

1 To kiss is to say, «I wish
2 to join our souls and bodies,»
3 and to lay together is to join souls
4 and bodies.

❤️ Lv 6

1 να βεν'εντο, ο-φαιλη νιε να
2 χαι'ε φαννω κιε'νω, καν φαιλη
3 να χαι'ε φαννω να λαγε κεν λϋω
4 να λιε λα νιεφαννω.

3 να δα λα κιε, ο-σε νοιγε να
4 κιε; ο-γαλη πο τεκ λη νεκ;
5 ο-ληλη πο τεκ λη βοι; ο-δαι
6 να κιε τια δοϋ δα γιωλη; ο-λϋω
7 λα να γε'ε ω'νω, βιει δα δαι
