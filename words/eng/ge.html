👤 Word of the Human 1

1 A person is all that lives and moves and which one
2 can see.

👤 Word of the Human 2

1 Every person stands as one before earth; there is nothing that
2 one person deserves and another does not.

👤 Word of the Human 3

1 Every person deserves food and drink and deserves the Word;
2 every person deserves love and company and serenity;
3 every person deserves the feelings of joy and pain and sadness and anger
4 and affection; every person who understands money deserves
5 enough money to sustain herself and live comfortably.
