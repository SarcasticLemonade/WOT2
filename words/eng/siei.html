⚖️ Ge 1

1 Evils count not when one makes the perpetrator
2 commit a crime, but when suffering was brought unto
3 another person, evil counts indeed.

⚖️ Ge 2

1 Good-will is the desire to accept good, and
2 an anti-desire to receive evils.

⚖️ Ge 3

1 Although you want to receive and take all that is good,
2 steal it not from others,
3 for others also want those good things, like
4 you do.
