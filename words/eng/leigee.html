📖 Br 1

1 Bright is not whosoever knows everything; bright
2 is whosoever desires and strives to know more
3 than what she knows. Whosoever is at peace with their
4 state of knowledge and does not desire to know more
5 is not bright.

📖 Br 2

1 Destroy not evils but its ingredients.

📖 Br 3

1 As animals gather food in the days of
2 plenty in order to prepare for winter and
3 hibernation, and as one builds their house when
4 it is dry in order to prepare for rain; one
5 shall make her beliefs in the days
6 of lucidity in order to prepare for days
7 of turmoil; she shall write her laws when
8 one is of clear mind in order to prepare for
9 occultation by ignorance or moods. As one builds not
10 her shelter against rain when it is raining, one builds not her shelter
11 against the moods whilst under the moods.
