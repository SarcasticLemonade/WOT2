🎭 Mo x+1

1 To sympathise with a person is to feel together with
2 them. To sympathise with someone is to be glad when
3 they are glad, and to be sad when they are
4 sad.

🎭 Mo x+2

1 Pity the hungry, for there is
2 no rejoicement when there is no food; pity
3 the bright, for there is no rejoicement when all
4 people upon the whole face of earth are ignorant;
5 pity those who have no love, for there is
6 no rejoicement when there is no person who shall love you
7 forevermore; pity all those who are
8 dead, for death is when one loses
9 all that is good, all that is evil, and when one loses earth.
