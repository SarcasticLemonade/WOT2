🥠 2 Sa 1

1 The sage said, "Come not
2 to see the day when wars erupt over that which
3 is not; come not to see the day when
4 children sell away their minds for flesh before
5 they learn how to speak; come not
6 to see the day when it be declared a crime to love;
7 come not to see the day when the brightest
8 of the bright know not what a tree is,"
9 said the sage.

🥠 2 Sa 2

1 The sage said the, "As earth doth
2 unto man, so man shalt do unto earth;
3 as earth doth unto man, so man shalt do
4 unto deer; for through love one thanketh
5 those who love her; through hatred one showeth
6 ingratitude to them who love her," said
7 the sage.
