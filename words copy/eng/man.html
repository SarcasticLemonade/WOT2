🎭 Md 1001

1 To empathise with someone is to feel together with
2 her. To empathise with someone is to be glad when
3 she is glad, and to be sad when she is
4 sad.

🎭 Md 1002

1 Pity the hungry, for there is
2 joy when food lacks; pity
3 the bright, for there is no joy when all
4 peoples upon the entire face of earth are ignorant;
5 pity the unloved, for there is
6 no joy when there is nobody who shall love thee
7 forevermore; pity all those who are
8 dead, for death is when one loseth
9 all that is good, all that is bad, and when one loseth earth.
