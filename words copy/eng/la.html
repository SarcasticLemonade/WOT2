❤️ Lv 1

1 There are three [kinds of] love: there is familial love,
2 there is neighbourly love and there is romantic love.
3 Romantic love is the will of all people
4 within a family to help one another and to do
5 good unto one another. Familial love must be [had] unless
6 one member of the family hath committed a grave sin [against them].
7 One must have and give neighbourly love unto each
8 person on earth, benefactor and malefactor,
9 sloth and weirdo. Romantic love one
10 must give unto one person all life long, for
11 this bond is such that one can
12 not make again with another person after one
13 hath already made it with someone.

❤️ Lv 2

1 A lover is someone who loveth and is loved.
2 Thy lover is whosoever loveth thee and whomsoever thou
3 lovest.

❤️ Lv 3

1 When thou wantest to do evil unto someone,
2 [reverse thy desire and] do good instead; when [thy] child hath broken
3 something, punish her not, but tell
4 her how to prevent that [from becoming in the future];
5 when someone hath something which thou desirest, steal
6 it not from her, but ponder and
7 think about how to restore peace [to thine and her mind] and forgive [let go of resentment for]
8 her.

❤️ Lv 4

1 If one partner loveth not the psyche of the other partner
2 but only her body, it is not love,
3 for [romantic; mostly] love adoreth the whole human and a whole
4 human is a psyche within a body.

❤️ Lv 5

1 To kiss [romantically] is to say, «I desire to
2 unite our psyches and bodies»
3 and to lay together is to unite their
4 psyches and their bodies.

❤️ Lv 6

1 During the act [of intercourse], tend not to
2 thy fleshly desires, but tend
3 to the fleshly desires of [thy] lover and give [express]
4 unto her non-fleshly love.

❤️ Lv 7

1 Love thyself, love every neighbour of thine
2 on earth, and love those who are not humans.
3 In order to love thyself, be a benefactor unto
4 thyself; eat after every major work;
5 drink after each minor work; give
6 unto thyself time to rest; show
7 love unto other people, for to give
8 goodness is to receive goodness and vice versa.

❤️ Lv 8

1 See never images of love before thou
2 receivest love; see never images of flesh
3 before thou seest [actual] flesh; see never the image
4 of intercourse before thou layest with another,
5 for whosoever came to belove the act's image before [partaking in] the act is
6 like whosoever laid with another before love; she shall
7 never feel in its entirety the blessing of love.
