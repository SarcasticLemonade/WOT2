🙉 Ig 1002

🙉 Ig 1003

1 Whosoever had ten burglars rob her is going
2 to have enough and to go and steal
3 from another ten people; whosoever had
4 no love shown for her for ten years is going to
5 show no love for others for another ten
6 years; knowing how to end this
7 cycle is the key to destroying many evils.

🙉 Ig 1004

1 The greatest goods of all will come to whomsoever craves
2 them not: food will come to the full;
3 money will come to the rich; power
4 will come to whomsoever craves it not; love
5 to the loveless; knowledge will come
6 to the bright.
