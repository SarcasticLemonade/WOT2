1 Life is the time during which one existeth upon
2 the face of earth. Life is all of earth, all that is good, all that is evil,
3 and all of time. Life is all that one is
4 to know, do and feel. There is no
5 time beyond [thy] life, yet to earth and other [people]
6 there is time beyond thy life. Life is the blessing
7 above all blessings, for life giveth
8 the might to see goodness, and [life] giveth unto
9 thee the might to see evils so that thou
10 might rejoice.
