⚖️ WGe 1

1 Evil counteth not when one maketh the wrongdoer
2 do her crimes, but when suffereth
3 another person, evil counteth.

⚖️ WGe 2

1 The moral desire is a willingness to take goodness for thyself, and
2 and unwillingness to receive evil.

⚖️ WGe 3

1 Although thou wantest to take and receive all goodness,
2 take it not from others,
3 for others also desire goodness, just like
4 thou.
