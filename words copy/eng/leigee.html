📖 Br 2

1 The bright one is not whosoever knoweth all things in all matters; bright
2 is one who still desireth to know more than
3 that which she already knoweth. Whosoever is content with that which she
4 knoweth and seeketh not wider knowledge
5 is not [truly] bright.

📖 Br 3+

1 Destroy not evil, but that which begetteth evil.

📖 Br 4+

1 As the beasts gather their food in the days of
2 plenty to be ready for winter and
3 sleep, and as one buildeth her shelter while
4 it is dry, that she may be ready for rain; one
5 shall make her beliefs in the hour of
6 clarity, that she may be ready for the hour of
7 clouding of mind; she shall write her laws, while
8 her mind is bright, that she may have them [by her side] when
9 her mind is shrouded by the moods; one doth not
10 build a shelter against rain in the midst of rain, [and so] one doth not
11 build a shelter against the moods while under the moods.
